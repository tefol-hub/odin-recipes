Description

A beef burger is a simple and tasty meal. You shape ground beef into a round patty and cook it on a hot grill or pan. Put the cooked meat inside a soft bun with fresh vegetables and your favorite sauces for a quick dinner.

You can make this juicy burger at home in just 15 minutes. It needs only basic items that you can find at any local store. Follow these easy steps to cook a great meal for your family tonight.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 cheese slices
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 sliced tomato
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup

Steps

  1. Shape the ground beef into four equal round patties.
  2. Sprinkle salt and pepper on both sides of each patty.
  3. Heat a pan or grill over medium-high heat.
  4. Cook the patties for 4 minutes on each side.
  5. Place a cheese slice on each patty during the last minute of cooking.
  6. Toast the hamburger buns lightly in the pan.
  7. Place the cooked beef and cheese on the bottom bun.
  8. Add lettuce, tomato, and ketchup, then cover with the top bun.
