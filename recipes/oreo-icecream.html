Oreo Icecream

Home

Description

Make delicious Oreo ice cream at home with just a few simple ingredients. You do not need a machine to make this sweet and creamy frozen treat.

Whip heavy cream until stiff peaks form. Gently fold in sweetened condensed milk and crushed Oreo cookies, then freeze the mixture until solid.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy whipping cream
  • 1 can (14 ounces) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 15 Oreo cookies (crushed)

Steps

  1. Whip the heavy cream and vanilla extract in a large bowl until stiff peaks form.
  2. Pour in the sweetened condensed milk.
  3. Fold the milk gently into the whipped cream so it stays fluffy.
  4. Add the crushed Oreo cookies into the mixture.
  5. Stir gently to spread the cookies evenly.
  6. Put the mix into a freezer-safe container.
  7. Freeze for at least 6 hours or until firm.
